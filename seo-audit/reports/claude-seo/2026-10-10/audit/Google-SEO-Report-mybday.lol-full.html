1. Executive Summary

This report presents a comprehensive SEO audit of mybday.lol for a Consumer web app with paid listings (birthday leaderboard) site, generated on October 11, 2026. Findings combine technical, content, schema, performance, visual, and search-readiness evidence as available.

2. Audit Categories

2.1 Technical SEO

Score: 84/100

What Works

  • 382 sitemap URLs, all 200, all self-canonical
  • Fully server-rendered; valid robots.txt; single-hop redirects
  • Five security headers on every response

Findings

Every page is sent no-store High

All public pages answer Cache-Control: private, no-cache, no-store, so a CDN can't cache anything.

Recommendation: Static/ISR for content pages; short revalidate plus revalidateTag from the Stripe webhook for boards.

404 body is empty without JavaScript Medium

Right status and noindex, but no message, links or lang in the HTML.

Recommendation: Add a server-rendered app/not-found.tsx.

About 240 KB gzipped JS/CSS on every page Medium

Radix UI loads on content-only pages such as Terms.

Recommendation: Lazy-load the Boost, Share and Gift menus and the date picker.

X-Powered-By header exposed Low

Responses carry X-Powered-By: Next.js.

Recommendation: Set poweredByHeader: false.

2.2 Content Quality

Score: 68/100

What Works

  • Facts verified by script on all 366 date pages: 0 errors
  • No filler or AI-pattern text (content_quality.py 91-92)
  • Cited commonness rank on every date page

Findings

Age label reads as wrong today Medium

The number is the age the person turns on the board's next date.

Recommendation: Label it 'Turns 48' (client's copy, 07 D12).

No holiday or observance context Medium

/december-25 never mentions Christmas; strict uniqueness is 37%.

Recommendation: A small holidays table feeding the About paragraph (client OK, 07 D9).

Placeholders on legal pages Medium

'[Launch date]' and '[If required: ... Article 27 GDPR.]' are visible.

Recommendation: Client fills them in or edits them in the admin.

Famous-people data bugs Info

Adult performers, duplicate dates, 'footballer' labels, cut-off labels and February's rarest date.

Recommendation: Fixed during the audit with tests; data refreshed.
